What Is AI Visibility (and Why Your Business Is Invisible to ChatGPT)

Your next customer isn't Googling "best dentist near me" and scrolling ten blue links. They're asking ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity — and picking from a shortlist of three. If your business isn't on that shortlist, you don't exist in that conversation.

Nona Digi · October 7, 2026 · 7 min read

The short definition

AI visibility is whether — and how often — AI assistants recommend your business when someone asks for what you sell. Traditional SEO asks: "Do I rank on Google?" AI visibility asks: "When someone asks ChatGPT for a recommendation, does it mention me?"

It's a different game with different rules. Google ranks pages; AI assistants synthesize answers. Google rewards keywords; AI assistants reward clear, citable, trustworthy information about your business. A page-one Google ranking does not guarantee a ChatGPT mention — and a business with no Google presence at all can still get recommended if the assistant finds the right signals.

We measured our own. The result was humbling.

We sell AI visibility services, so we ran the same benchmark we run for clients: 35 unbranded questions an owner might ask an AI assistant — "who should I hire for AI digital marketing," "what are the best AI receptionist companies in Orlando," "how can AI help my small business handle missed calls." Each asked fresh, no brand names, default search mode.

Zero. Not once. The assistants recommended the same shortlist every time — national agencies, established software brands, local competitors with years of reviews behind them. We're publishing this because it's the honest starting point every local business faces: the AI shortlist is already written, and you're probably not on it.

The encouraging half: when we asked 16 questions about our business directly, the assistant answered all of them accurately — services, pricing, Orlando focus — citing our website every time. The foundation (clear site, structured data, honest copy) works for retrieval. What's missing is recommendation: being the answer, not just an accurate result.

How AI visibility is actually measured

There's no single dashboard for this yet, so measurement means structured testing. Here's the framework we use — five checks:

  1. AI visibility: ask unbranded discovery questions across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Are you mentioned? Recommended? In what position?
  2. Google presence: your Business Profile, reviews, and local pack standing — still the raw material assistants draw on.
  3. Website fundamentals: can AI crawlers read your site? Is there an llms.txt file, schema markup, clean structure?
  4. After-hours capture: when the assistant's user clicks through at 11 PM, does someone answer? (This is the one-time lens we check during an audit — not nightly monitoring.)
  5. Review velocity: are real customers leaving recent reviews, in volume? Assistants treat recency and volume as trust signals.

Run the same prompts monthly and you have a trend line — the closest thing this industry has to a ranking report.

The levers that actually move it

No one can guarantee a ChatGPT recommendation — any agency promising that is selling you something. But the inputs are knowable, and they're mostly unglamorous:

  • Be citable. Publish clear, specific pages about what you do, what it costs, and who it's for. Vague marketing copy gives assistants nothing to quote. (This blog exists for exactly that reason.)
  • Structure your site for machines. Schema markup, an llms.txt file, and clean crawlability tell AI crawlers what your business is. Google's own guidance says no special AI-search steps are required — just a well-built site.
  • Earn reviews continuously. Zero real reviews is the single most common reason an accurate business still doesn't get recommended. Assistants hedge when there's no independent evidence.
  • Keep your Google Business Profile complete. It's still the canonical record assistants check first.
  • Say the quiet parts out loud. "We're a chatbot, not a phone system" is exactly the kind of honest, specific claim assistants can verify and repeat. We renamed our own product to AI Chat Receptionist for this reason.

The one-sentence version: AI visibility is earned the boring way — a clear website, real reviews, structured data, and specific claims an assistant can verify. There are no shortcuts, which is exactly why most of your competitors haven't done it.

Why this matters more for local businesses

National brands win AI recommendations on sheer volume of mentions. Local businesses win on specificity: the assistant recommending "a dentist in Lake Nona who books online and answers in Spanish" needs local, specific facts. Most local competitors have thin websites, three reviews from 2021, and no structured data. The bar is low — which means the first business in a market to take this seriously has a real head start.

That's the opportunity, and it's time-limited. Every month the shortlists get more entrenched.
