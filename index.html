Questions, answered

Fair questions.

Will the AI sound like a robot?

No — it's trained on your actual services, pricing, and tone of voice, and it hands off to you the moment a conversation needs a human. You approve every answer pattern before it goes live.

What if it gives a wrong answer?

Guardrails first: it only answers from your approved knowledge base, says "let me check with the team" when unsure, and you get a transcript of every conversation to review.

Is this just another chatbot?

Under the hood, yes — it's an AI chatbot. But a generic chatbot answers a few FAQs and stops. Ours does the full front-desk job: it answers in your voice, qualifies leads, and books appointments straight into your calendar, 24/7. That's why we call it an AI Receptionist.

Why only two services?

Because we'd rather do two things brilliantly than seven things adequately. These are the two levers that move revenue fastest for local businesses — everything else we add only when it earns its place.
