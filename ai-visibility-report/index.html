What it is

What is an AI Visibility Report?

It's a five-point audit of how your business shows up where customers now look first: inside AI assistants. We ask the questions your customers actually ask, document what each platform says about you — and about your competitors — and hand you an honest report in plain English.

No jargon, no automated scan. A person does the checks, writes the findings, and records you a personal walkthrough video.

Why it matters

What does "AI visibility" mean?

AI visibility means being the business an AI assistant recommends when someone asks "who's the best dentist near me?" — not just a blue link on page two, but the named answer in the conversation.

Why it matters: customers increasingly skip search results entirely and act on the AI's recommendation. If you're invisible there, you're invisible at the exact moment they decide.

Improving it

How do you improve
a company's AI visibility?

Four levers, in order of impact. This is exactly what our AI Visibility Engine does every month.

01

Say citable things

AI recommends businesses it can describe precisely. Clear services, real pricing, specific locations — published where AI reads.

02

Earn reviews weekly

AI assistants quote review counts and ratings as evidence. A steady stream beats a five-year-old 5.0.

03

Fix the fundamentals

Fast, mobile-friendly, easy to book. AI won't recommend a business whose website frustrates the customer it sends.

04

Track it monthly

Re-measure inside the AI platforms, watch competitors, and adjust. Visibility compounds — or decays.
