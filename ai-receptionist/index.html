FAQ

The objections, answered.

How is this different from the chatbot on my website now?

Most website chatbots are deflection machines — menus, dead ends, "please call us during business hours." An AI chat receptionist is trained on your actual business and completes the job: answers the real question, qualifies the lead, books the appointment.

Will it replace my front-desk staff?

No — it covers what they can't: nights, weekends, lunch hours, and the second call that comes in while they're helping the first customer. Most owners keep their staff and stop losing the calls staff never heard.

What if a customer gets frustrated?

It recognizes frustration, stays calm, and hands off to you with a full summary — name, number, and exactly what went wrong. You follow up once, informed, instead of discovering a bad review later.

Who owns the conversation data?

You do. Every conversation, lead, and booking belongs to your business. If you ever cancel, your data goes with you.

What happens if I cancel?

There are no long-term contracts. If you cancel, the chat receptionist stops answering and your data is handed over. The founding rate lock simply ends.
